- Our derived calendar data (filtered country/region selections, the common EU dates and added Canadian federal rules) remains available under CC BY-SA 3.0. The exported HTML includes calendar attribution and a license link.

The reference calendar includes national/federal and identified banking holidays. US/Canadian federal days do not establish a day off for every employee. Great Britain uses England/Wales; India includes the core national calendar. Common EU dates are January 1 and December 25, not an institution-wide or member-state-wide extended calendar. Regional, special or one-off declarations and changes to lunar holiday dates may require an official local source.
